=== B. What happens after a shock of k x ATR? ===
For every bar whose range >= k x ATR(14,M1) and which closed DOWN (or UP), the average move of the
close over the next h minutes, in $ per 0.01 lot, with its t and the share of positive outcomes.
A positive number after a DOWN bar means the drop gets bought back (fade works); after an UP bar a
positive number means the move continues. 'cost' is the spread that has to be paid to take it.
